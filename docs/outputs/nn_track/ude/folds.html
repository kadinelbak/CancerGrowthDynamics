Stage 2b — hold-out folds

What this is

Each fold refits the models without some training drug wells and predicts those wells from their nominal seeding (no refit). The intermittent folds test an unseen dose schedule — what optimal control relies on; the 1 µM fold an unseen dose between tested doses; the 20k fold an unseen seeding density. The UDE and the pure neural ODE are refitted from 5 seeds each (mean ± SD over seeds); Hill and no drug are deterministic single fits. The learned-resource UDE is not refitted here (runtime). Runtime: 62.0 min (fits reused from checkpoints where present). Back to Stage 2.
