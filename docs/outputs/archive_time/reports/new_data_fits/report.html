Generated 2026-09-27 23:41 with GrowthParameterEstimation.run_joint_fit on the Mean Cells trajectories in New Datasets. Candidate models come from the Candidate models page. At each stage every candidate is fitted, the candidates are ranked by BIC, and the stated parameters are carried into the next stage.

  • Stage 1 · Untreated monocultures (A2780 = sensitive N, A2780cis = resistant C) → growth rate r and carrying capacity K per line.
  • Stage 1b · Low-resource monocultures (30k sheets): inherit r and K; fit the low-resource terms.
  • Stage 2 · Treated monocultures: inherit r and K; the three doses of each line are fitted jointly with shared drug parameters; EC50 is fixed at 1 µM.
  • Stage 3 · Untreated (Ce0) and treated (Ce1) cocultures together: all three seeding ratios and both conditions (12 trajectories) are fitted at once with one shared carrying capacity K; Ce1 is 1 µM cisplatin.
  • Stage 3b · Low-resource cocultures (30k, runs 1 and 2): inherit K and α from Stage 3 and d^LR from Stage 1b.

How to compare models: each fit shows a BIC chart and a comparison table for all candidates. Below them, the Show fit for buttons switch the plot, the fitted equation (with the estimated values substituted) and the parameter table to any candidate; All candidates overlaid draws every candidate on one plot. ΔBIC evidence bands: < 2 weak, 2–6 positive, 6–10 strong, > 10 very strong.

Low-resource data: only A2780 / A2780cis 30k sheets from ALL A2780-cis LR data.xlsx are used (Tyknu is not). FBS is 10% in normal medium (\(u_R = 0\), so Stages 1–3 have no low-resource term) and 0.5% in low resource, so \(u_R = (10-0.5)/(10-0.05) = 0.9548\) in Stages 1b and 3b. Sudden transient drops are excluded from the fit (a point below half of the previous kept value, by more than 50 cells, after which the trajectory recovers to at least 80% of that value; steady declines and spikes are kept), as are values that exactly repeat an earlier day's value (copy/paste errors); each excluded point is drawn as a grey × and replaced by a hollow diamond placed on the fitted line afterwards. All excluded points are listed at the end. Doses are read from the file names: IC25 file = 1.47 µM, IC50 file = 1 µM, IC75 file = 0.62 µM. Points are observations; lines are model fits. All plots share the same axes (0–14 days, 0–4800 cells).

Stage 1b — Low-resource monoculture

\[\text{Lineage-specific subtractive loss ★:}\quad \frac{dX}{dt}=r_X X\left(1-\frac{X}{K_X}\right)-d_X^{LR}\,u_R(t)\,X,\qquad u_R=\frac{10-F}{10-0.05}\]
\[\text{Growth-rate and carrying-capacity modifiers:}\quad \frac{dX}{dt}=\rho_r r_X X\left(1-\frac{X}{\rho_K K_X}\right)\]
\[\text{Separately fitted growth rate:}\quad \frac{dX}{dt}=r_X^{LR} X\left(1-\frac{X}{K_X}\right)\]
\[\text{Accumulated damage, 4 steps (delayed loss):}\quad \frac{dX}{dt}=r_X X\left(1-\frac{X}{K_X}\right)-d_X^{LR}\,u_R\,A_4(t)\,X,\qquad A_n(t)=1-e^{-\lambda_X^{LR} t}\sum_{k=0}^{n-1}\frac{(\lambda_X^{LR} t)^k}{k!}\]
\[\text{Immediate loss + accumulated damage, } n = 4 \text{ or } 8:\quad \frac{dX}{dt}=r_X X\left(1-\frac{X}{K_X}\right)-\left(d_X^{LR}+d_X^{acc}A_n(t)\right)u_R\,X\]

Accumulated damage: cells pass through \(n\) damage steps at rate \(\lambda\) before the extra loss acts (mean delay \(n/\lambda\)), the transit-compartment idea used for delayed drug effects (Lobo & Balthasar 2002; Simeoni et al. 2004).

\(r_X, K_X\) are inherited from Stage 1. Sheets: 1 - mono A2780 30k and 1 - mono A2780cis 30k. Carried forward: \(d_X^{LR}\) from the ★ model.

Carried forward: \(\lambda_X\) and \(h_X\).
