Bootstrapping — model selection that favours fewer terms

Generated 2026-09-28 10:08. Every candidate from the New data fitting report is refitted to 200 bootstrap replicates of its data, plus pruned candidates that remove terms (Stage 2: no delay; Stage 3b: no low-resource loss).

  • Replicates: the most complex candidate's fit plus its residuals, resampled in circular blocks of 3 days within each trajectory so day-to-day correlation is kept; day 0 stays as observed.
  • Parsimony rule (per replicate): take the lowest-BIC candidate, then prefer any simpler candidate whose BIC is no more than 3 per removed parameter higher (removing two terms may cost up to 6 BIC). Among eligible models the one with the fewest parameters wins.
  • Bootstrap-optimal model: the candidate the rule picks most often. Its parameters get 95% bootstrap percentile intervals; a parameter whose interval reaches zero is flagged as removable.

Inherited parameters are held at their point estimates, so upstream uncertainty is not carried forward; the bootstrap intervals describe each stage on its own. Low-resource fits use the same excluded points as the fitting report.
