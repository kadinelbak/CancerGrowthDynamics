Validation — low-resource + cisplatin coculture

Generated 2026-09-29 10:18. No parameters are fitted here. A2780 and A2780cis were co-cultured 1:1 at 30k in low resource (0.5% FBS, \(u_R = 0.955\)) with cisplatin, assumed at the 1 µM IC50 used for Ce1. This condition was never used for fitting. Every prediction combines parameters already estimated in the New data fitting report; what varies is how the ingredients are assumed to act and interact. Each prediction starts from the day-1 counts (N = 1073.5, C = 250.9) and is scored on days 2–14: the day-0 values (49.9 each, SD 0) are the nominal seeding count, and the measured counts jump 22-fold (A2780) and 5-fold (A2780cis) by day 1, beyond any fitted growth rate.

Files: New Datasets/Validation/ALL A2780 LR + cis(A2780 30k 1-1).csv and ALL A2780 LR + cis(A2780cis 30k 1-1).csv (mean of 3 wells per day).

Scoring: with no free parameters, BIC = n ln(SSE/n), so ranking by BIC is ranking by squared error. R² compares with a flat line at each population's mean (below 0 = worse than that line). The naive reference "both populations stay at their day-1 counts" scores BIC 272.96; a ✓ marks combinations that beat it. The same exclusion rule as the fitting report removes 3 transient drops (marked ×).

How the terms can interact

\[\frac{dN}{dt}=\underbrace{\rho_r\,r_N\,g_{\text{drug}}\,N\left(1-\frac{N+\alpha_{NC}C}{\rho_K K}\right)}_{\text{growth}}-\underbrace{\left(d_N+d_N^{LR}u_R\right)N}_{\text{death}}-\underbrace{\beta_N A_N(t)\,H_N(D)\,s_N\,N}_{\text{drug kill}}\]

The same form holds for C with its own parameters. Four ingredients are switched between options, each option using values from an earlier stage:

Capacity and crowding
K and α from the Stage 3 or Stage 3b fits (optimal or shared-K-only); the Stage 3 optimal also brings its sensitive death rate d_N.
How low resource acts
not at all · as an extra death rate \(d^{LR}u_R\) · as a lower growth rate and capacity (\(\rho_r, \rho_K\)) · as a lower growth rate only · as an immediate loss plus extra loss that accumulates through 8 damage steps (delayed death; the best Stage 1b model for A2780cis). These are Stage 1b candidates.
How cisplatin acts
not at all · kills after a delay \(A(t)=1-e^{-\lambda t}\) (Stage 2 or Stage 3 fit) · kills from day 0 (\(A=1\)) · stops growth instead of killing: \(g_{\text{drug}}=\max(0,\,1-\beta A H/r)\) and no kill term.
Drug × growth interaction
independent (\(s=1\)): the kill rate is the same whatever the conditions · dividing cells only (\(s = \max(0, 1-\text{crowding})\)): the drug only kills cells with room to divide, so crowding and starvation protect cells. This is the usual picture for cisplatin.

Resource-depletion model search (medium changed every other day)

Each of 96 combinations of mechanisms is fitted once, jointly, to all 22 training cultures (untreated, low-resource and treated monocultures, Ce0 + Ce1 cocultures and low-resource cocultures; 414 points after day 1) with one shared parameter set in which resource level, dose and co-culture are conditions. Each fitted model then predicts this held-out culture with no refit. Training data and prediction both start at day 1.

Why: the constant-effect models below cannot produce a rise followed by a crash. Here each culture's medium carries a nutrient level \(R\) (fresh 10% FBS medium = 1) that both lines consume; the medium is replaced on days 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12, resetting \(R\). Low resource simply starts each fresh medium lower. Cisplatin is assumed re-added with each change (constant dose). Supported by work showing batch-culture nutrients run out within days and that serum starvation sensitises cancer cells to cisplatin.

Why no model can predict this culture from the training data: no training culture combines low resource and cisplatin. Any term for how the two interact, such as the starvation–drug amplification \(\gamma\), leaves the training error unchanged, so the training data cannot pin it down: across the fitted models its value spans the whole allowed range (0 to 10). The validation culture is the first time the combination appears, and there A2780 is suppressed far more than by either condition alone. Predicting it needs at least one training culture with both, or a calibration of \(\gamma\) on part of the validation data.

\[\frac{dN}{dt}=r_N\,g(f)\,N\left(1-\frac{N+\alpha_{NC}C}{K}\right)-\delta_N(1-f)\,N-\beta_N A_N(t)H_N(D)\left(1+\gamma(1-f)\right)N,\qquad \frac{dR}{dt}=-\frac{q_N N+q_C C}{1000}\,\frac{R}{R+K_R}\]

\(f=R(1+K_R)/(R+K_R)\) is nutrient availability (1 in fresh normal medium). Options: crowding one-for-one or asymmetric × uptake the same per cell or per line × starvation kills (\(g=1\)), slows growth (\(g=f\), no \(\delta\)) or both × delayed or immediate kill × drug independent of nutrients or amplified by starvation (\(\gamma\)) × low-resource medium fixed at 5% of normal (0.5/10% FBS) or fitted. Fitting: bounded differential evolution (BlackBoxOptim), 50000 evaluations per model.

Result: the best fit to the training data (shared K + asymmetric α · uptake per line · starvation kills · immediate kill · drug independent of nutrients · LR medium = 5% of normal; 14 parameters) scores validation BIC 297.38 (R² -14.97 sensitive, -0.56 resistant; validation rank 69 of 96). The best validation score is 276.75 (shared K, one-for-one · uptake per line · starvation kills · delayed kill · starvation amplifies drug · LR medium level fitted; training rank 31). 0 of 96 fitted models beat the no-change reference (BIC 272.96). Rank correlation between training and validation performance: ρ = 0.48.

Constant-effect model search, fitted on all training data

Each of 192 combinations of mechanisms is fitted once, jointly, to all 22 training cultures (untreated, low-resource and treated monocultures, Ce0 + Ce1 cocultures and low-resource cocultures; 414 points after day 1) with one shared parameter set in which resource level, dose and co-culture are conditions. Each fitted model then predicts this held-out culture with no refit. Training data and prediction both start at day 1.

Mechanism options: crowding (shared K one-for-one, shared K with asymmetric α, or separate K per line with α) × low resource (extra death, slower growth \(r(1-\rho u_R)\), lower capacity \(K(1-\kappa u_R)\), or both) × cisplatin (delayed kill, immediate kill, or delayed growth arrest; Hill dose response with EC50 = 1 µM) × drug–resource interaction (independent, low resource amplifies the drug by \(1+\gamma u_R\), or kills dividing cells only) × baseline death (none or \(d_N, d_C\)). Low resource acts as a constant condition. Fitting: bounded BFGS for all, then Nelder–Mead polishing of the 30 best by training BIC.

Result: the best fit to the training data (shared K, one-for-one · LR: extra death · drug: delayed kill · kills dividing cells only · no baseline death; 11 parameters) scores validation BIC 283.76 (R² -6.89 sensitive, -0.34 resistant; validation rank 61 of 192). The best validation score is 255.69 (shared K + asymmetric α · LR: extra death · drug: delayed growth arrest · LR amplifies drug · no baseline death; training rank 131). 15 of 192 fitted models beat the no-change reference (BIC 272.96). Rank correlation between training and validation performance: ρ = 0.48.
