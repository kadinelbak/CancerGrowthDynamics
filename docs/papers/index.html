From Culture to Control

Staged mechanistic modeling and optimal dosing of cisplatin-sensitive and -resistant ovarian cancer cells, and a transportability study between stages.

  • A2780 / A2780cis growth, low resource, cisplatin and coculture, fitted stage by stage from a model zoo
  • Qualification: bootstrap, sensitivity, profile likelihood, Sobol indices and held-out validation
  • Model predictive control of the cisplatin schedule, with fasting as a second control
  • 46 numbered equations, each with a plain-language explanation; interactive plots for growth laws, drug delays, the Hill kill, coculture dosing, BIC, barriers and fasting
