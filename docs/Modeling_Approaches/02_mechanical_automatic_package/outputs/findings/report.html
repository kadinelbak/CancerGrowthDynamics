What did not carry over between conditions

  • Resistant kill in coculture. The best coculture model without any cisplatin term for A2780cis has BIC 637.7; the best with one 642.8 (Δ -5.1). The carried model is cc_full_noC: in the treated cocultures cisplatin has no detectable effect on A2780cis at 1 µM. In monoculture the A2780cis kill is needed: without it the Stage 2b fit is +31.4 BIC worse. The monoculture drug response therefore does not transfer to coculture.
  • Low resource + cisplatin (Stage 4). The model assembled from the earlier stages, with nothing refitted, is +27.6 BIC worse than the carried s4_chi (χ = 1.15). The drug and low-resource mechanisms fitted separately do not combine additively.
  • Seeding scale of the low-resource experiments. 15 of the 16 low-resource and combination series start 1.5–10.5-fold above their nominal seeding (median 3.2): their first counts are far beyond one day of growth, so those plates were seeded or imaged at a different effective density. Their starting values are estimated; their capacity and low-resource effects should be compared with the normal-medium experiments with this in mind. The start of co_lowres_run2_25S75R_A2780 sits at its lower bound (0.1 × nominal): the model does not fit that series' early counts.

Limitations and the experiments that would resolve them

  • One biological run per condition (two for low resource); residuals are correlated day to day (shared imaging artefacts), so intervals are conditional and probably too narrow.
  • The treated cocultures informed the choice of weighting and drug model; the intermittent-dosing data are the only untouched validation set.
  • The cocultures were treated at one dose only; the coculture dose response and the resistant kill in coculture rest on that single dose. Experiment: a coculture dose series (e.g. 0.25, 0.5, 2, 4 µM).
  • Recovery from starvation stress after refeeding was never measured and drives the fasting result. Experiment: fast-then-refeed cultures with daily counts.
  • No data beyond day 14; long control runs are extrapolations without passaging. Experiment: 28-day cultures with media changes.
  • The low-resource plates start far above their nominal seeding (see above). Check: the seeding density and imaging field of those plates.
  • The 30k Ce0 75:25 resistant series reads about 0.8× of the raw-image rebuild; it is used as supplied.
