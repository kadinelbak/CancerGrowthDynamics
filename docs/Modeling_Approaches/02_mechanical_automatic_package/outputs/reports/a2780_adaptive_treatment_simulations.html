Data-selected model and scope

The reproduction tab displays saved predictions from the winning model for each condition. The schedule sandbox starts from the winning untreated-coculture model: logistic growth for Naive, theta-logistic growth for total A2780cis, asymmetric competition, and lineage-specific death. The values below are the shared center of the fitted 20k/30k model. Only the schedule sandbox splits cis into exploratory high-kill \(S\) and low-kill \(T\) response components.

\[G_N(N,L_N)=r_NN\left(1-\frac{L_N}{K_N}\right),\qquad r_N=0.872,\quad K_N=4032\]
\[G_C(C,L_C)=r_CC\left[1-\left(\frac{L_C}{K_C}\right)^{\theta_C}\right],\qquad r_C=0.487,\quad K_C=3803,\quad \theta_C=3.084\]
\[C=S+T\]

The observable cis population is always \(C(t)\). Internal \(S(t)\) and \(T(t)\) states support the selected equation but are not separately measured populations. They remain hidden unless the advanced diagnostic is enabled.

Advanced scenario controls can apply explicit multipliers without changing the stored default:

\[r_N^{*}=m_r r_N,\qquad r_C^{*}=m_r r_C,\qquad K_N^{*}=m_K K_N,\qquad K_C^{*}=m_K K_C\]

Here \(m_r\) and \(m_K\) are hypothetical scaling assumptions. They are not estimates of human tumor growth or human carrying capacity.

Each lineage feels an effective coculture load:

\[L_N=N+\alpha_{NC}C,\qquad L_C=C+\alpha_{CN}N,\qquad \alpha_{NC}=0.566,\quad \alpha_{CN}=1.221\]

The fitted density-independent losses are \(d_N=0.204\ \mathrm{day}^{-1}\) and \(d_C=0.080\ \mathrm{day}^{-1}\). The optional slider adds a common exploratory turnover \(\delta\), which defaults to zero.

The administration command \(u_{cmd}(t)\) is set by dose-specific windows. Experimental doses are mapped through the measured 0.67, 1.0, and 1.47 uM levels. Intermediate values are interpolation; values outside that range are extrapolation. The fitted experimental protocol uses the same constant effect level used during parameter estimation from day 0 through the observed endpoint; the recorded two-day media refresh maintains that condition. Effective exposure \(E(t)\) begins to decay only after treatment stops in schedule experiments.

\[k_{decay}=\frac{\ln 2}{t_{1/2}},\qquad \frac{dE}{dt}=k_{decay}\left[u_{cmd}(t)-E\right]\]
\[\frac{dc_i}{dt}=k_{in}E-k_{out}c_i,\qquad \frac{dc_k}{dt}=k_{bind}c_i-k_{repair}c_k\]

The data-selected default uses the fitted Hill effects and timing activations:

\[H_N(E)=\frac{E_{\max,N}E^{h_N}}{EC_{50,N}^{h_N}+E^{h_N}},\qquad H_{Cj}(E)=\frac{E_{\max,Cj}E^4}{0.5^4+E^4},\quad j\in\{S,T\}\]
\[M_N=\exp(\beta_NL_N/K_N),\qquad M_C=\exp(\beta_CL_C/K_C),\qquad \rho_T=\exp(\gamma_{r,T})\]
\[\frac{dN}{dt}=G_N(N,L_N)-(d_N+\delta)N-M_NA_N(t)H_N(E)N\]
\[\frac{dS}{dt}=G_C(C,L_C)\frac{S}{C}-(d_C+\delta)S-M_CA_C(t)H_{CS}(E)S\]
\[\frac{dT}{dt}=\rho_TG_C(C,L_C)\frac{T}{C}-(d_C+\delta)T-M_CA_C(t)H_{CT}(E)T\]

The intracellular states \(c_i\) and \(c_k\) above are used only when the El-Kareh and Secomb-inspired advanced PK/PD candidate is selected. The default model does not silently inherit those unfitted constants.

Adaptive schedules evaluate their threshold rule only at \(t_m=m\tau_{monitor}\), then hold the chosen dose until the next observation. This prevents the browser model from assuming continuous, perfectly informed clinical decisions.

Day 14 is the end of calibration. Later predictions are extrapolation and are shown with an ensemble sensitivity band. Reversible phenotype switching is default-off and advanced-only; it never represents A2780Naive converting into the established A2780cis cell line.
