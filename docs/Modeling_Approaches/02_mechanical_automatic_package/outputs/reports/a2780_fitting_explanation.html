Fitting Explanation

An explanation-first hierarchy for A2780 modeling. Intrinsic growth is deliberately restricted to logistic or theta-logistic form; treatment, competition, death, and coculture context are introduced only in the stage where they are tested.

Interpretation status: this is a constrained teaching hierarchy, not a replacement for the canonical evidence-selected ranking. Downstream stages must be refitted before constrained BIC values can be reported.
The governing rule

Start simple and add mechanisms only when the experiment introduces them

1. Baseline growthLogistic or theta-logistic only
2. Add treatmentDose response, onset, delay, damage, or latent drug response
3. Add cocultureDirectional competition and lineage-specific loss
4. Combine contextsOnly extra treated-coculture terms
Strong simplicity preference. Logistic is fitted first. Theta-logistic is adopted only when its BIC improvement is at least 2 units and its parameters are finite. Lag, adaptation, death, and interaction candidates remain Stage 1 sensitivity diagnostics and are not inherited by this explanatory hierarchy.
How fitting works

One objective across trajectories, with scale and complexity controlled

\[\mathrm{SSE}_{\mathrm{scaled}}=\sum_j\sum_t\left[\frac{y_j(t)-\widehat y_j(t)}{s_j}\right]^2,\qquad s_j=\max_t y_j(t)\]
\[\mathrm{BIC}=n\ln\left(\frac{\mathrm{SSE}_{\mathrm{scaled}}}{n}\right)+k\ln n\]
Trajectory scaling
Each trajectory is divided by its observed peak so high-count curves do not dominate solely because of their units.
Joint fitting
Both seeding densities are fitted together instead of estimating unrelated biology for every curve.
Fixed initial state
Measured day-zero populations are anchors, not kinetic parameters or negative-time shifts.
Parameter counting
Every freely optimized parameter contributes to \(k\); fixed inherited parameters are not counted again.
Fit validity
Non-finite parameters, predictions, or objectives and failure sentinels are rejected before ranking.
Stage 1

Untreated monoculture: intrinsic growth only

Allowed: \(r\), \(K\), and optionally \(\theta\). Deferred: time delay, adaptation, death, drug response, competition, and coculture modifiers.

Logistic baseline

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=rX\left(1-\frac{X}{K}\right)\]

Growth is approximately exponential when \(X\ll K\), slows with density, and reaches zero net growth at \(X=K\).

Theta-logistic baseline

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=rX\left[1-\left(\frac{X}{K}\right)^\theta\right]\]

The shape parameter \(\theta\) changes how sharply density dependence appears. Logistic growth is the special case \(\theta=1\).

Stage 2

Treated monoculture: add drug-specific behavior

Fixed: Stage 1 growth. Estimated here: dose response, onset, gradual activation, damage clearance, or differential drug response.

\[\frac{dX_i}{dt}=G_i(X_i)-A_i(t)H_i(z)X_i\]
\[H_i(z)=\frac{E_{\max,i}z^{h_i}}{EC_{50,i}^{h_i}+z^{h_i}},\qquad A_i(t)=\mathbf 1_{t>t_{\mathrm{on},i}}\left[1-e^{-\lambda_i(t-t_{\mathrm{on},i})}\right]\]

The Hill term answers how strongly a dose acts; activation answers when it acts. Transit-damage and sensitive/tolerant candidates belong here because they are treatment-response hypotheses.

Stage 3

Untreated coculture: add interaction and loss

Fixed: each lineage's Stage 1 growth. Estimated here: directional competition and lineage-specific loss.

\[L_N=N+\alpha_{NC}C,\qquad L_C=C+\alpha_{CN}N\]
\[\frac{dN}{dt}=G_N(N,L_N)-d_NN,\qquad \frac{dC}{dt}=G_C(C,L_C)-d_CC\]

Competition can be asymmetric because \(\alpha_{NC}\) and \(\alpha_{CN}\) may differ. Loss is introduced here because decline occurs in coculture; it is not used to reshape monoculture baseline growth.

Stage 4

Treated coculture: test only the additional context effect

Inherited: Stage 1 growth, Stage 2 treatment, and Stage 3 interaction. Estimated here: only extra changes required when treatment and coculture occur together.

\[C=S+T,\qquad M_N=e^{\beta_NL_N/K_N},\qquad M_C=e^{\beta_CL_C/K_C}\]
\[\frac{dN}{dt}=G_N^{\mathrm{co}}-M_NA_N(t)H_N(z)N\]
\[\frac{dS}{dt}=G_C^{\mathrm{co}}\frac{S}{C}-M_CA_C(t)H_{CS}(z)S\]
\[\frac{dT}{dt}=\rho_TG_C^{\mathrm{co}}\frac{T}{C}-M_CA_C(t)H_{CT}^{\mathrm{co}}(z)T\]

The measured cis output is always \(C=S+T\). The internal \(S\) and \(T\) states are treatment-response hypotheses, not separately observed populations.

Scientific boundary

What may be concluded now

  • The simple Stage 1 rule selects theta-logistic growth for both cell lines.
  • Naive's Baranyi modification is effectively unnecessary by BIC; cis adaptation has measurable support and remains an omitted early-time feature.
  • Current Stage 2–4 families show where later effects belong, but their numerical BIC values cannot be reused after changing Stage 1 inheritance.
  • A completed constrained analysis requires sequentially refitting Stages 2, 3, and 4 and regenerating all diagnostics.
Do not say: “There is no lag.” Say: “For interpretability, intrinsic growth uses theta-logistic baselines; residual early-time mismatch is retained as a documented limitation and sensitivity result.”
