Why these models were selected

The shortlist follows the current BIC artifacts while preserving a low-complexity ladder: a minimal reference, a strong low-parameter alternative, and the best still-interpretable candidate where needed. A literature benchmark is labeled as diagnostic when it is not eligible for staged inheritance. BIC values are comparable only within the same fitted objective, never across stages or cell-line objectives.

Why the registry is organized this way

Frequently used controls and equations remain visible. States, parameter names, and interpretation notes use one details disclosure because they are secondary during scanning. Advanced and legacy registries require an explicit scope change. This follows progressive-disclosure guidance, GOV.UK details guidance, and USWDS guidance for structured comparison lists.
