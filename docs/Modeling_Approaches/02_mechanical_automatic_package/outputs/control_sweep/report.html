Every variant is scored with its own thresholds, and every comparator is rerun under it.

Barriers. relaxed_log: the relaxed log barrier (Feller & Ebenbauer 2017); quadratic: zero below B_cancer, a quadratic penalty above; hard: a near-wall that makes any interval at or above B_cancer cost about 1000.0, so a crossing plan is chosen only when every plan crosses; none: no burden penalty, the progression step only.

Read with care. Runs longer than 14 days (shaded) follow the fitted equations beyond the data. A green cell marks an MPC that progresses later than every comparator. Scenario A (the main result) has no detectable resistant kill in coculture, so no cost design can kill resistant cells there: killing sensitive cells frees room for resistant ones. The coculture kill factors (φ) were measured at 1 µM only, the one dose the cocultures received, and are applied unchanged at every dose, so scenario A at 0.62 and 1.47 µM extrapolates them; scenario B is the bound for a resistant kill as in monoculture. Everything past day 14 is model extrapolation: the experiments end at day 14. Scenario B is a what-if: the monoculture drug response applied in coculture. The treated cocultures contradict it: at a constant 1 µM it predicts a day-14 total of 592 cells at 50:50 against 3310 measured (scenario A, the Stage 3a coculture fit: 3398).
