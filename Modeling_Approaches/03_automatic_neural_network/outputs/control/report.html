Stage 5 — optimal dose scheduling

Set-up

Same problem as OptimalControlOne: 13 daily doses, mean dose fixed at 1.0 uM by the same shift-and-clamp projection, minimise total cells at day 13. Model: recovered closed form (kill_S = v + v³ + v²·ρ; kill_R = v²), 8-member bootstrap ensemble; objective = mean + 0.5·sd of log final total across members (robust). Optimiser: multistart L-BFGS with exact gradients; BlackBoxOptim cross-check. Each schedule is also scored under the UDE and Hill ensembles.

Results

rel_to_constant = final total relative to constant 1 uM (closed form). support flags doses or exposure patterns the training data never covered; treat those predictions as extrapolation.
