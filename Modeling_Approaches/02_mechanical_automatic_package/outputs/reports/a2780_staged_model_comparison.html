Stage 2 notation key

Summary

Equation progression

The selected models build sequentially. Every growth, density, dose, delay, population-balance, competition, death, context-scaling, initial-condition, observation, and model-selection term used by the winning pipeline is defined below.

Design, density pooling, and initial conditions

\[s_d=\begin{cases}-1,&d=20\mathrm{k}\\+1,&d=30\mathrm{k}\end{cases}\]
\[p_d=p_{\mathrm{center}}e^{s_d\delta_p},\qquad |\delta_p|\le \ln(1.05)\]
\[U_d=\begin{cases}67,&d=20\mathrm{k}\\100,&d=30\mathrm{k}\end{cases}\]
\[X_i(0)=U_d\qquad\text{(monoculture cell line }i\text{)}\]
\[N(0)=f_{\mathrm{mix}}U_d,\qquad C(0)=(1-f_{\mathrm{mix}})U_d\qquad\text{(coculture)}\]
\[S(0)=(1-f_{T0})C(0),\qquad T(0)=f_{T0}C(0)\]
\[y_N(t)=N(t),\qquad y_C(t)=C(t)=S(t)+T(t)\]
\[[x]_+=\max(x,0)\]

The density contrast is symmetric and bounded at plus-or-minus five percent. Untreated partial pooling applies it to r and K; treated partial pooling applies it to treatment amplitudes; untreated coculture partial pooling applies it to both competition coefficients together. Starting populations are fixed and are never kinetic parameters.

1. Intrinsic untreated monoculture growth

\[\frac{dN}{dt}=r_{N,d}N\left[1-\frac{N}{K_{N,d}}\right]_+\qquad\text{(A2780Naive)}\]
\[\frac{dC}{dt}=r_CC\left[1-\left(\frac{C}{K_C}\right)^{\theta_C}\right]_+\qquad\text{(A2780cis)}\]

The selected winners are density-partially-pooled logistic growth for A2780Naive and shared theta-logistic growth for A2780cis. These exact families and effective parameters are inherited by all later stages.

2. Add treated-monoculture effects

\[z=\begin{cases}0.25,&D=0.67\,\mu\mathrm{M}\;(\mathrm{IC}_{25})\\0.50,&D=1.00\,\mu\mathrm{M}\;(\mathrm{IC}_{50})\\0.75,&D=1.47\,\mu\mathrm{M}\;(\mathrm{IC}_{75})\end{cases}\]
\[A_N(t)=\mathbf{1}_{t>t_{\mathrm{on},N}}\left[1-e^{-\lambda_N(t-t_{\mathrm{on},N})}\right]\]
\[A_C(t)=\mathbf{1}_{t>t_{\mathrm{on},C}}\]
\[\varepsilon=10^{-12}\]
\[H_{N,d}(z)=\frac{E_{\max,N,d}z^{h_N}}{EC_{50,N}^{h_N}+z^{h_N}+\varepsilon}\]
\[H_{CS,d}(z)=\frac{E_{\max,CS,d}z^4}{0.5^4+z^4+\varepsilon},\qquad H_{CT,d}(z)=\frac{E_{\max,CT,d}z^4}{0.5^4+z^4+\varepsilon}\]
\[E_{\max,i,d}=E_{\max,i,\mathrm{center}}e^{s_d\delta_{E,i}},\qquad |\delta_{E,i}|\le\ln(1.05)\]
\[\frac{dN}{dt}=r_{N,d}N\left[1-\frac{N}{K_{N,d}}\right]_+-A_N(t)H_{N,d}(z)N\]
\[C=S+T\]
\[\frac{dS}{dt}=r_CC\left[1-\left(\frac{C}{K_C}\right)^{\theta_C}\right]_+\frac{S}{C}-A_C(t)H_{CS,d}(z)S\]
\[\frac{dT}{dt}=r_CC\left[1-\left(\frac{C}{K_C}\right)^{\theta_C}\right]_+\frac{T}{C}-A_C(t)H_{CT,d}(z)T\]

The A2780Naive winner is delayed Hill-ramp kill. The A2780cis winner has separate sensitive and tolerant kill amplitudes and an estimated initial tolerant fraction. A five-model timing audit selected cis_onset_only by BIC; onset, gradual activation, shared-onset, and bounded-onset alternatives remain in the ranking rather than being silently discarded.

3. Add untreated-coculture competition and death

\[C=S+T\]
\[\alpha_{NC,d}=\alpha_{NC,\mathrm{center}}e^{s_d\delta_\alpha},\qquad \alpha_{CN,d}=\alpha_{CN,\mathrm{center}}e^{s_d\delta_\alpha}\]
\[L_N=N+\alpha_{NC,d}C,\qquad L_C=C+\alpha_{CN,d}N\]
\[G_N^{\mathrm{co}}=r_{N,d}N\left(1-\frac{L_N}{K_{N,d}}\right)-d_NN\]
\[G_C^{\mathrm{co}}=r_CC\left[1-\left(\frac{L_C}{K_C}\right)^{\theta_C}\right]-d_CC\]
\[\frac{dN}{dt}=G_N^{\mathrm{co}},\qquad \frac{dC}{dt}=G_C^{\mathrm{co}}\qquad\text{(untreated coculture)}\]

The winner is asymmetric competition with lineage-specific death. The two competition coefficients may differ by direction; their common density contrast is bounded at plus-or-minus five percent. Death rates are shared across density. Monoculture r, K, and theta remain fixed.

4. Fully expanded treated-coculture winner

\[D=1.0\,\mu\mathrm{M}=\mathrm{IC}_{50},\qquad z=0.50\]
\[M_N=\exp\!\left(\operatorname{clamp}\!\left[\beta_N\frac{L_N}{K_{N,d}},-4,4\right]\right),\qquad M_C=\exp\!\left(\operatorname{clamp}\!\left[\beta_C\frac{L_C}{K_C},-4,4\right]\right)\]
\[f_{T0}^{\mathrm{co}}=\operatorname{logit}^{-1}\!\left[\operatorname{logit}(f_{T0})+\delta_f\right]\]
\[H_{CT,d}^{\mathrm{co}}(0.50)=e^{\gamma_T}H_{CT,d}(0.50),\qquad \rho_T=e^{\gamma_{r,T}}\]
\[\frac{dN}{dt}=r_{N,d}N\left(1-\frac{L_N}{K_{N,d}}\right)-d_NN-M_NA_N(t)H_{N,d}(0.50)N\]
\[\frac{dS}{dt}=\left\{r_CC\left[1-\left(\frac{L_C}{K_C}\right)^{\theta_C}\right]-d_CC\right\}\frac{S}{C}-M_CA_C(t)H_{CS,d}(0.50)S\]
\[\frac{dT}{dt}=\rho_T\left\{r_CC\left[1-\left(\frac{L_C}{K_C}\right)^{\theta_C}\right]-d_CC\right\}\frac{T}{C}-M_CA_C(t)H_{CT,d}^{\mathrm{co}}(0.50)T\]
\[C=S+T,\qquad L_N=N+\alpha_{NC,d}C,\qquad L_C=C+\alpha_{CN,d}N\]

This is the selected load_plus_tolerant_growth_context model. Intrinsic growth and untreated competition/death parameters are fixed from Stages 1 and 3. Stage 2 drug parameters and the BIC-selected timing architecture are inherited within a plus-or-minus five-percent validation window. The fitted context terms are total-load drug scaling beta_N,beta_C, a resistant tolerant-fraction shift delta_f, tolerant-kill attenuation gamma_T, and a tolerant-growth plasticity multiplier rho_T.

Fitting and BIC selection

\[s_j=\max_t y_j(t)\]
\[\mathrm{SSE}_{\mathrm{scaled}}=\sum_j\sum_t\left[\frac{y_j(t)-\widehat y_j(t)}{s_j}\right]^2\]
\[\mathrm{BIC}=n\ln\!\left(\frac{\mathrm{SSE}_{\mathrm{scaled}}}{n}\right)+k\ln n\]

Every candidate is fitted jointly across the environments stated in its stage. Here n is the total number of observations and k counts all fitted center, shape, modifier, and pooling-contrast parameters. Fixed inherited parameters and fixed day-zero populations are not counted again.

Symbol and function guide

This glossary states what every symbol does biologically and mathematically. A symbol with subscript d can differ between 20k and 30k only through the explicitly bounded density-pooling rule.

Populations and indices

\(t\)
Time in days from the fixed day-zero initial condition.
\(X_i(t)\)
Generic total population for a one-state monoculture model of cell line \(i\). This symbol avoids assigning a biological compartment that the model does not contain.
\(N(t)\)
A2780Naive population when both lineages are modeled explicitly.
\(P_i(t),D_i(t)\)
Proliferating/live and damaged-visible states used only in the transit-compartment model.
\(S(t),T(t)\)
Latent drug-sensitive and drug-tolerant A2780cis subpopulations used only in the population-balance model.
\(C(t)=S(t)+T(t)\)
Total observed A2780cis population. The experiment does not separately observe \(S\) and \(T\).
\(\widehat y_i(t)\)
Model prediction on the observed scale. It equals \(X_i\), \(P_i+\tfrac12D_i\), or \(S+T\), according to model structure.
\(\dot X=dX/dt\)
Derivative notation for a state's rate of change. Different letters mean distinct biological states, not inconsistent names for one quantity.
\(i\)
Lineage index: \(N\) for A2780Naive or \(C\) for A2780cis.
\(d\)
Starting-density environment: 20k or 30k.

Intrinsic growth

r_i
Intrinsic per-capita growth rate, with units day^-1.
K_i
Carrying capacity on the measured cell-count scale.
theta_i
Dimensionless theta-logistic shape. \(\theta=1\) recovers ordinary logistic growth; other values shift how sharply crowding suppresses growth.
[x]_+
Positive part \([x]_+=\max(x,0)\), used in monoculture fits to prevent the inherited growth term from becoming artificial negative crowding death above K.

Starting density and mixture

U_d
Fixed total at day zero: 67 for 20k and 100 for 30k.
f_mix
Nominal A2780Naive fraction from the 25:75, 50:50, or 75:25 mixture label.
f_T0
Estimated initial tolerant fraction within the A2780cis population.
s_d
Density sign, -1 for 20k and +1 for 30k, used to create symmetric deviations around a cell-line center.
delta_p
Log-scale density contrast bounded by \(|\delta_p|\le\ln(1.05)\), so an eligible effective parameter can deviate by at most five percent in either direction.

Drug dose and Hill response

D
Physical drug concentration in uM.
z
Normalized IC effect level: 0.25, 0.50, or 0.75 for IC25, IC50, or IC75.
Emax_i
Maximum per-capita drug-effect amplitude, with units day^-1.
EC50_i
Effect level or concentration at which the Hill term reaches half of Emax.
h_i
Dimensionless Hill coefficient controlling dose-response steepness.
H_i(z)
Hill dose-response function. It converts dose/effect level into a potential per-capita kill or damage rate; it does not encode treatment delay.

Treatment activation A(t)

\[A_N(t)=\mathbf{1}_{t>t_{\mathrm{on},N}}\left[1-e^{-\lambda_N(t-t_{\mathrm{on},N})}\right]\]
\[A_C(t)=\mathbf{1}_{t>t_{\mathrm{on},C}}\]
A_i(t)
Dimensionless fraction of the eventual treatment effect active at time t. The Naive lineage uses a delayed ramp. The resistant lineage uses the BIC-selected cis_onset_only timing rule.
t_onset,i
Delay in days before drug action starts.
lambda_i
Activation rate in day^-1. Larger values switch treatment on faster. After onset, the activation half-time is \(\ln(2)/\lambda_i\).
\(k_i(t,z)=A_i(t)H_i(z)\)
Actual time- and dose-dependent per-capita treatment rate applied to the population.

Coculture interaction

alpha_NC
Effect of one cis cell on the crowding experienced by A2780Naive.
alpha_CN
Effect of one A2780Naive cell on the crowding experienced by A2780cis.
\(L_N=N+\alpha_{NC}C\)
Effective competitive load perceived by A2780Naive.
\(L_C=C+\alpha_{CN}N\)
Effective competitive load perceived by A2780cis.
d_N,d_C
Additional lineage-specific first-order loss rates in day^-1 in the competition-with-death model.

Coculture treatment modifier

beta_N,beta_C
Dimensionless coefficients linking normalized competitive load to drug-effect scaling.
M_i
Dimensionless multiplier on inherited drug action. \(M_i=1\) means no coculture modification, above one strengthens drug action, and below one weakens it.
delta_f
Log-odds shift in the latent tolerant fraction specifically in treated coculture: \(f_{T0}^{\mathrm{co}}=\operatorname{logit}^{-1}[\operatorname{logit}(f_{T0})+\delta_f]\).
gamma_T
Log multiplier on tolerant-compartment drug kill in coculture. Its exponential directly scales H_CT.
rho_T
Positive multiplier on tolerant-compartment growth in treated coculture. It equals exp(gamma_r,T); the selected estimate is approximately 1.25.
clamp(x,-4,4)
Numerical/biological guard limiting the log multiplier before exponentiation.
Current sign
The fitted beta_N and beta_C are negative, so larger competitive load attenuates effective drug action in the selected model.

How every candidate model works

Stage 1: untreated monoculture candidates

Logistic

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=rX\left[1-\frac{X}{K}\right]_+\]

Growth is nearly exponential at low population and slows linearly with occupancy \(X/K\). It has the fewest mechanistic parameters.

Theta-logistic

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=rX\left[1-\left(\frac{X}{K}\right)^\theta\right]_+\]

Adds a shape parameter. This can move most growth suppression closer to or farther from carrying capacity without changing the low-density growth rate.

Gompertz

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=rX\ln\!\left(\frac{K}{X}\right)\]

Per-capita growth decreases logarithmically with population, producing asymmetric sigmoidal trajectories.

Simple-death diagnostic

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=rX\left[1-\frac{X}{K}\right]_+-dX\]

Tests whether an untreated first-order loss term is needed. It is diagnostic and is inherited only with strong identifiable BIC support.

Allee diagnostic

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=rX\left[1-\frac{X}{K}\right]_+\left(\frac{X}{A_{\mathrm{Allee}}}-1\right)\]

Tests for suppressed or negative growth below a critical population threshold. It is not treated as a default baseline.

Pooling: shared and partial-pooling versions use the same biological ODE. Partial pooling adds bounded density contrasts to r and K; independent fits are diagnostic only.

Stage 2: treated monoculture candidates

Anchored linear kill

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=G(X)-k_{\mathrm{kill}}DX\]

Immediate kill proportional to physical concentration. It is simple but cannot create a delayed rise-then-decline trajectory.

Anchored concentration-Hill kill

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=G(X)-\frac{E_{\max}D^h}{EC_{50}^h+D^h}X\]

Immediate saturating concentration response. It remains monotonic in time at fixed dose.

Time-decay kill

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=G(X)-k_{\mathrm{kill}}De^{-\lambda t}X\]

Drug action is strongest initially and wanes over time, representing clearance, adaptation, or loss of exposure and allowing later regrowth.

Immediate IC-Hill

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=G(X)-H(z)X\]

Uses normalized IC effect levels rather than physical concentration, with no onset delay.

Hill ramp

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=G(X)-\left(1-e^{-\lambda t}\right)H(z)X\]

Drug action accumulates from day zero.

Delayed Hill ramp

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=G(X)-A(t)H(z)X\]

Adds an explicit onset time before the accumulating effect. This is the selected A2780Naive mechanism.

Transit damage

\[\frac{dP}{dt}=G(P)-A(t)H(z)P,\qquad \frac{dD}{dt}=A(t)H(z)P-k_{\mathrm{clear}}D\]
\[\widehat y=P+\frac12D\]

Cells leave the proliferating live compartment, remain partly visible while damaged, and are cleared later. The candidate fixes Hill EC50=0.5 and h=4.

Sensitive/tolerant populations

\[\frac{dS}{dt}=G(S+T)\frac{S}{S+T}-A_C(t)H_{CS}(z)S,\qquad \frac{dT}{dt}=G(S+T)\frac{T}{S+T}-A_C(t)H_{CT}(z)T\]
\[\widehat y=S+T\]

Both latent states share one inherited total-growth law but have different kill amplitudes. Their initial split is controlled by f_T0. This is the selected A2780cis mechanism.

Timing audit: independent onset plus ramp; shared onset plus ramp; onset differences bounded within 0.5 day; resistant gradual-only; and resistant onset-only.

These timing candidates refit the same 12-trajectory objective through GrowthParameterEstimation.run_joint_multistart. BIC therefore compares the timing architectures with their different parameter counts on an identical data set.

Stage 3: untreated coculture candidates

Symmetric competition

\[\alpha_{NC}=\alpha_{CN}=\alpha,\qquad \frac{dN}{dt}=G_N(N+\alpha C),\qquad \frac{dC}{dt}=G_C(C+\alpha N)\]

Both lineages exert the same cross-crowding strength.

Asymmetric competition

\[\alpha_{NC}\ne\alpha_{CN},\qquad \frac{dN}{dt}=G_N(N+\alpha_{NC}C),\qquad \frac{dC}{dt}=G_C(C+\alpha_{CN}N)\]

Each lineage can affect the other differently.

Asymmetric competition plus death

\[\frac{dN}{dt}=G_N(L_N)-d_NN,\qquad \frac{dC}{dt}=G_C(L_C)-d_CC\]

Adds lineage-specific loss beyond competitive growth suppression. This is the selected Stage 3 model. All six density/mix environments are fitted together.

Stage 4: linked treated-coculture hypotheses

Strict inheritance

\[M_N=M_C=1\]

The same intrinsic drug vector acts in monoculture and coculture with no context modification.

Competitor-scaled

\[M_N=e^{\beta_N\alpha_{NC}C/K_N},\qquad M_C=e^{\beta_C\alpha_{CN}N/K_C}\]

Only the opposing lineage's contribution modifies drug action.

Load-scaled

\[M_N=e^{\beta_NL_N/K_N},\qquad M_C=e^{\beta_CL_C/K_C}\]

Total effective load, including self and competitor, modifies drug action. This is the BIC winner.

Load plus context amplitude

\[M_i=e^{\beta_iL_i/K_i+\gamma_i}\]

Adds a density-independent coculture shift gamma_i on top of load scaling.

Tolerant context shift

\[f_{T0}^{\mathrm{co}}=\operatorname{logit}^{-1}[\operatorname{logit}(f_{T0})+\delta_f],\qquad H_{CT}^{\mathrm{co}}=e^{\gamma_T}H_{CT}\]

Allows coculture to enrich the latent tolerant state and attenuate its kill rate while preserving the stage-2 sensitive/tolerant model.

Subpopulation load scaling

\[M_{CS}=e^{\beta_{CS}L_C/K_C},\qquad M_{CT}=e^{\beta_{CT}L_C/K_C}\]

Tests whether competitive load scales sensitive and tolerant kill differently without changing their initial composition.

Load plus tolerant context

\[M_N,M_C,\delta_f,\gamma_T\quad\text{jointly define load scaling and tolerant-state modification}\]

Combines total-load drug scaling with the resistant-state shift.

Load plus tolerant growth context

\[M_C=e^{\beta_C\,L_C/K_C},\qquad \left.\frac{dT}{dt}\right|_{\mathrm{growth}}=\rho_TG_C^{\mathrm{co}}\frac{T}{C},\qquad \rho_T=e^{\gamma_{r,T}}\]

Adds one bounded phenotype-specific growth-plasticity multiplier to the preceding model. This is the selected Stage 4 mechanism; BIC pays for the additional fitted parameter.

Fully free context diagnostic

\[\mathbf q_{\mathrm{drug}}^{\mathrm{co}}\ne\mathbf q_{\mathrm{drug}}^{\mathrm{mono}}\]

Fits separate complete drug vectors by context. It tests whether inheritance fails, but is deliberately diagnostic because it gives up mechanistic sharing.

Important: Stage 4 does not attach an arbitrary coefficient to every term. Growth and untreated interaction parameters are fixed from earlier stages, and the Stage 2 drug vector is restricted to a plus-or-minus five-percent inheritance window. Candidate-specific context terms are counted in BIC, and the fully free context vector remains diagnostic only.

How the four-stage handoff works

  1. Stage 1 learns intrinsic growth. Each cell line is fitted jointly across 20k and 30k. Its winning family and effective r/K/theta values become the biological growth baseline.
  2. Stage 2 learns treatment response. The Stage 1 growth law is fixed while all three monoculture doses and both densities determine delayed, Hill, transit, or population-balance parameters. A second joint BIC audit chooses whether resistant treatment needs onset, gradual activation, both, or timing shared with Naive.
  3. Stage 3 learns untreated interaction. Stage 1 growth remains fixed while both densities and all mixture ratios determine competition and death parameters.
  4. Stage 4 combines the mechanisms. Stage 1 growth and Stage 3 interaction are fixed. Stage 2 drug parameters are inherited within plus-or-minus five percent. BIC then tests strict inheritance, load effects, resistant-state shifts, tolerant growth plasticity, and a fully separate diagnostic vector across the combined treated data.
