A2780 Low-Resource Modeling Plan

Untreated Monoculture

Logistic

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=r_X X\left(1-\frac{X}{K_X}\right)\]

Theta Logistic

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=r_X X\left[1-\left(\frac{X}{K_X}\right)^{\theta_X}\right]\]

Gompertz

\[\frac{dX}{dt}=r_X X\log\left(\frac{K_X}{X}\right)\]

Notation

\(X(t)\)
Measured population of the monoculture cell line.
\(r_X\)
Cell-line growth-rate parameter.
\(K_X\)
Cell-line carrying capacity in the assay.
\(\theta_X\)
Theta-logistic shape parameter.

Treated Monoculture

Time-Delayed Hill Function

\[ A_X(t)=1-e^{-\lambda_X t},\qquad t\ge0 \] \[ H_X(D)=\frac{D^{h_X}}{EC_{50,X}^{h_X}+D^{h_X}} \] \[ \frac{dX}{dt}=r_X X\left[1-\left(\frac{X}{K_X}\right)^{\theta_X}\right]-\beta_XA_X(t)H_X(D)X \]

Drug Effect Distributed Into Logistic Growth

\[ \text{Normal logistic form:}\qquad \frac{dX}{dt}=r_X\,X\left(1-\frac{X}{K_X}\right)-\beta_X\,A_X(t)\,H_X(D)\,X \]
\[ \text{Distributed form:}\qquad \frac{dX}{dt}=\underbrace{r_X\,X}_{\text{unrestricted growth}} -\underbrace{\frac{r_X}{K_X}X^2}_{\text{density-dependent loss}} -\underbrace{\beta_X\,A_X(t)\,H_X(D)\,X}_{\text{drug-associated loss}} \]
\[ \text{Normal logistic without Hill saturation:}\qquad \frac{dX}{dt}=r_X\,X\left(1-\frac{X}{K_X}\right)-\beta_X\,A_X(t)\,D\,X \] \[ \text{Distributed without Hill saturation:}\qquad \frac{dX}{dt}=r_X\,X-\frac{r_X}{K_X}X^2-\beta_X\,A_X(t)\,D\,X \]

Notation

\(D\)
Cisplatin concentration.
\(A_X(t)\)
Exponential delay multiplier between 0 and 1.
\(\lambda_X\)
Rate at which the effect rises continuously from zero toward full strength.
\(H_X(D)\)
Bounded Hill transformation of dose between 0 and 1.
\(\beta_X\)
Drug-effect parameter.
\(EC_{50,X}\)
Concentration producing half of the maximum Hill effect.
\(h_X\)
Hill-curve shape parameter.

Untreated Coculture (optional stage)

Asymmetric Crowding

\[ \frac{dN}{dt}=r_NN\left[1-\left(\frac{N+\alpha_{NC}C}{K_N}\right)^{\theta_N}\right] \] \[ \frac{dC}{dt}=r_CC\left[1-\left(\frac{C+\alpha_{CN}N}{K_C}\right)^{\theta_C}\right] \]

Asymmetric Crowding Plus Death

\[ \frac{dN}{dt}=r_NN\left[1-\left(\frac{N+\alpha_{NC}C}{K_N}\right)^{\theta_N}\right]-d_NN \] \[ \frac{dC}{dt}=r_CC\left[1-\left(\frac{C+\alpha_{CN}N}{K_C}\right)^{\theta_C}\right]-d_CC \]

Notation

\(N(t),C(t)\)
Measured A2780Naive and total A2780cis populations.
\(\alpha_{NC}\)
Effect of A2780cis crowding on A2780Naive.
\(\alpha_{CN}\)
Effect of A2780Naive crowding on A2780cis.
\(d_N,d_C\)
Lineage-specific death or loss-rate parameters.

Treated Coculture

Asymmetric Crowding Plus Time-Delayed Hill Function

\[ \frac{dN}{dt}=r_NN\left[1-\left(\frac{N+\alpha_{NC}C}{K_N}\right)^{\theta_N}\right]-d_NN-\beta_NA_N(t)H_N(D)N \] \[ \frac{dC}{dt}=r_CC\left[1-\left(\frac{C+\alpha_{CN}N}{K_C}\right)^{\theta_C}\right]-d_CC-\beta_CA_C(t)H_C(D)C \]

Notation

\(A_N(t),A_C(t)\)
Inherited lineage-specific exponential delay multipliers.
\(H_N(D),H_C(D)\)
Lineage-specific Hill dose-response functions.
\(\beta_N,\beta_C\)
Lineage-specific drug-effect parameters.

Low Resource

Growth-Rate And Carrying-Capacity Modifiers

\[ \frac{dX}{dt}=\rho_r r_X X\left[1-\left(\frac{X}{\rho_KK_X}\right)^{\theta_X}\right] \]

Separately Fitted Growth Rate

\[ \frac{dX}{dt}=r_X^{LR}X\left[1-\left(\frac{X}{K_X}\right)^{\theta_X}\right] \]

Notation

\(\rho_r\)
Low-resource multiplier on the inherited growth rate.
\(\rho_K\)
Low-resource multiplier on the inherited carrying capacity.
\(r_X^{LR}\)
Growth rate fitted directly to the low-resource environment.

\[ \frac{dX}{dt}=\rho_r r_XX\left[1-\left(\frac{X}{\rho_KK_X}\right)^{\theta_X}\right]-\beta_XA_X(t)H_X(D)X \]

Notation

\(\rho_r,\rho_K\)
Low-resource growth-rate and carrying-capacity modifiers.
\(A_X(t),H_X(D)\)
Inherited exponential delay and Hill dose-response functions.
\(\beta_X\)
Inherited drug-effect parameter.
