Fitted Baseline And Exploratory Schedule Model

The reproduction tab displays saved predictions from the winning model for each condition. The schedule sandbox inherits the fitted untreated growth laws: logistic growth for Naive and theta-logistic growth for total A2780cis. Only the schedule sandbox splits cis into exploratory high-kill \(S\) and low-kill \(T\) response components.

\[G_N=r_NN\left(1-\frac{N}{K_N}\right),\qquad r_N=0.872,\quad K_N=4032\]
\[G_C=r_CC\left[1-\left(\frac{C}{K_C}\right)^{\theta_C}\right],\qquad r_C=0.487,\quad K_C=3803,\quad \theta_C=3.084\]
\[C=S+T\]

Each lineage feels an effective coculture load:

\[L_N=N+\alpha_{NC}C,\qquad L_C=C+\alpha_{CN}N\]

Treatment intensity \(u(t)\) ranges from 0 to 1 and scales kill pressure. The original reference schedule refreshes media/treatment every 2 days, so the default reference simulation uses a two-day refresh interval.

\[\frac{dN}{dt}=G_N(N,L_N)-u(t)k_NN\]
\[\frac{dS}{dt}=G_C(C,L_C)\frac{S}{C}-u(t)k_SS-q_{ST}u(t)S\]
\[\frac{dT}{dt}=\rho_TG_C(C,L_C)\frac{T}{C}-u(t)k_TT+q_{ST}u(t)S\]

The fitted-reproduction curves contain no added \(q_{ST}\) mechanism. In the schedule sandbox, \(q_{ST}\) is an optional hypothetical stress-induced shift and \(\rho_T\) is applied only under treatment in coculture. Schedule results remain exploratory until those terms and treatment waveforms are experimentally fitted or validated.
