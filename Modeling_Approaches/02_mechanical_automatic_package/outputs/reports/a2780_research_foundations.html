How To Read The Evidence

The page separates mathematical precedent from biological evidence so that a good fit is not mistaken for mechanistic proof.

Established model classThe equation is standard and has been tested on tumor-growth or pharmacodynamic data.
Ovarian evidenceA primary ovarian study directly supports the biological effect or scheduling concept.
Cross-cancer precedentThe mechanism has clinical or experimental support in another cancer, especially prostate cancer.
Project-specific hypothesisThe exact A2780 term is a testable parameterization introduced by this project.

Biological Grounding

What ovarian research supports

Platinum-sensitive and platinum-resistant ovarian cells can change one another's growth and drug response in direct coculture. Coculture experiments support direction-dependent interactions, while the peer-reviewed Hockings study shows a resistant fitness deficit under low-resource conditions. These observations justify testing interaction and density-context models, but they do not uniquely select one competition equation. [7] [19]

Ovarian treatment response can also be delayed by cell-state transitions. An OVCAR3/OVCAR4 PARP-inhibitor study selected a generalized-logistic untreated law and a cycling-to-arrested compartment model with a Hill dose response. Simeoni et al. provide the general transit-compartment precedent used as a comparison model. [4] [3]

A2780 and resistant derivatives are valid experimental systems for platinum response, while stromal context can alter apparent sensitivity. Bicaku et al. independently show substantial platinum-response heterogeneity across ovarian cell lines. Together these sources support keeping context effects in the candidate set rather than treating a monoculture drug coefficient as universal. [9] [21]

What prostate research contributes

Prostate adaptive therapy supplies the clearest patient-level precedent for threshold-guided treatment holidays. The abiraterone pilot used tumor-burden feedback to stop and restart therapy, and later analysis linked outcomes to sensitive/resistant competition and pretreatment composition. These studies support the simulator's adaptive-scheduling logic, not its ovarian parameter values. [12] [13]

What remains unproven here

The labels sensitive-like and tolerant-like are latent mathematical states unless lineage-resolved markers, sorting, or single-cell measurements distinguish them. Likewise, the fitted exponential load modifier says that drug action covaries with modeled load; it does not identify the molecular pathway responsible for that covariance.

How the foundational papers changed the project

The cisplatin pharmacodynamics paper motivated a new fitted diagnostic candidate with intracellular and DNA-bound platinum states. The turnover paper motivated an explicit background-loss control and resistant-fitness control in the schedule sandbox. The adaptive-timing preprint motivated discrete monitoring times rather than continuously updated treatment decisions. The ovarian cell-line study strengthens the use of dose-response heterogeneity but does not identify hidden states from total counts. The systems-model framework was added as validation guidance, not as biological evidence. [20] [15] [22] [21] [23]

Additional papers cited below supply equations, comparison models, ovarian context, clinical precedent, and fitting methodology. They supplement this foundational corpus rather than replacing it.

Fitting, Pooling, BIC, And Identifiability

Raw SSE
\(\mathrm{SSE}_{raw}=\sum_j\sum_t[y_j(t)-\hat y_j(t)]^2\). This is reported in original measurement units but allows large trajectories to dominate.
Trajectory-scaled SSE
\(\mathrm{SSE}_{scaled}=\sum_j\sum_t[(y_j(t)-\hat y_j(t))/s_j]^2\). The first sum runs over trajectories \(j\); the second runs over observation times within each trajectory. Scaling makes differently sized trajectories more comparable.
Joint fitting
All relevant trajectories contribute to one objective. Shared parameters represent a common cell-line mechanism; partial pooling permits only prespecified, bounded condition differences.
BIC
\(\mathrm{BIC}=n\ln(\mathrm{SSE}_{scaled}/n)+k\ln n\). Lower is preferred. The joint count \(k\) must include every shared and trajectory-specific fitted parameter exactly once. Schwarz introduced the criterion; its use here is an approximate Gaussian-error comparison, not proof that the winning mechanism is true. [17]
Multistart fitting
Repeated optimization from dispersed initial guesses tests whether the same optimum is recovered. Agreement raises confidence in numerical stability but cannot repair structural non-identifiability. [18]
Profile bounds
One parameter is fixed across a grid while all others are reoptimized. A bounded, bowl-shaped profile supports practical identifiability; a flat or one-sided profile warns that many parameter values explain the data similarly. [16]
Parameter stability
Variation across successful multistart fits detects weakly constrained directions. Stable predictions can coexist with unstable individual parameters.
Fixed \(u_0\) and time zero
The measured starting population is an initial condition, not a kinetic parameter. Changing seeding density changes the trajectory from day 0; it does not require interpreting unobserved negative days.
Staged inheritance
Untreated growth is learned before drug response; interaction is learned before treated coculture context. This reduces confounding, but uncertainty from earlier stages should ultimately be propagated rather than treating point estimates as exact.
Model credibility
The validation record should state the model's intended question, parameter sources, numerical verification, calibration data, predictive checks, sensitivity, identifiability, and limitations. The Braakman framework supports this evaluation workflow; it does not validate any A2780 mechanism by itself. [23]

Most important identifiability lesson: a low BIC can select the best predictive candidate in the tested set while the biological names attached to hidden states remain unproven.

What Must Change Before Human-Tumor Simulation

A larger carrying capacity can create a longer growth horizon, but simply raising the dish-scale \(K\) does not produce a clinically calibrated tumor. At least five new layers are required:

  1. Units and observation model: connect cells or confluence to tumor volume, imaging burden, ctDNA, CA-125, or another measured clinical signal.
  2. Patient-scale growth calibration: estimate growth and turnover from longitudinal human data; do not transfer plate-day parameters unchanged.
  3. Pharmacokinetics and pharmacodynamics: replace a media-refresh waveform with drug concentration, clearance, tissue penetration, and dose-limiting constraints.
  4. Spatial and environmental capacity: vascular supply, immune/stromal interactions, and metastatic sites make carrying capacity dynamic rather than one fixed plate limit.
  5. Prospective validation: freeze the model and predict new schedules or new experiments before using it to rank therapies.

Prostate adaptive therapy demonstrates that patient-specific feedback control is feasible when a longitudinal biomarker is available. Ovarian studies show encouraging preclinical adaptive-therapy results, but the appropriate ovarian feedback marker, drug exposure model, and stopping rules still require disease-specific validation.
