What did not carry over between conditions

  • Low resource + cisplatin (Stage 4). The model assembled from the earlier stages, with nothing refitted, is +11.9 BIC worse than the carried s4_eps (ε_N^R = 1.3, ε_C^R = 0.519). The drug and low-resource mechanisms fitted separately do not combine additively.
  • Seeding scale of the low-resource experiments. 15 of the 16 low-resource and combination series start 3.2–15.9-fold above their nominal seeding (median 6.5): their first counts are far beyond one day of growth, so those plates were seeded or imaged at a different effective density. Their starting values are estimated; their capacity and low-resource effects should be compared with the normal-medium experiments with this in mind. The start of co_lowres_run2_25S75R_A2780 sits at its lower bound (0.1 × nominal): the model does not fit that series' early counts.

Limitations and the experiments that would resolve them

  • One biological run per condition (two for low resource); residuals are correlated day to day (shared imaging artefacts), so intervals are conditional and probably too narrow.
  • The treated cocultures informed the choice of weighting and drug model; the intermittent-dosing data are the only untouched validation set.
  • The cocultures were treated at one dose only; the coculture dose response and the resistant kill in coculture rest on that single dose. Experiment: a coculture dose series (e.g. 0.25, 0.5, 2, 4 µM).
  • Recovery from starvation stress after refeeding was never measured and drives the fasting result. Experiment: fast-then-refeed cultures with daily counts.
  • No data beyond day 14; long control runs are extrapolations without passaging. Experiment: 28-day cultures with media changes.
  • The low-resource plates start far above their nominal seeding (see above). Check: the seeding density and imaging field of those plates.
  • The 30k Ce0 75:25 resistant series reads about 0.8× of the raw-image rebuild; it is used as supplied.
