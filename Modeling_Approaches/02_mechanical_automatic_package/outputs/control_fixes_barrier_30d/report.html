Generated 2026-11-24 04:03 by run_control_fasting.jl and control_fasting_report.jl. Model-predictive control now chooses two things at every media change (every 2 days) for 30 days: the cisplatin dose and whether the next 2 days are run in low resource ("fasting", 0.5% FBS). The goal is the same as in the 14-day report: the longest time to progression (keep N + C under B_prog = 0.5 K, 80% confluence), with the resistant cells left at the end of each look-ahead priced and a soft burden penalty above B_cancer; MPC minimises this weighted cost. Fasting has a host cost (body weight, with a 5% weight-loss limit) and a host benefit (protection of healthy tissue from the drug), both informed by the fasting-and-chemotherapy literature. Every run starts from the nominal seeding (100 cells split by the ratio). The look-ahead is 28 days at every decision, also near day 30 (the plan runs past it), re-planned every 2 days, so the schedule is the first 15 decisions of a continuing policy. Scenarios A and B are as in the 14-day report.

Time to progression first, and runs stop once the answer is known (user, 2026-11-01). The progression term charges the time spent at or above B_prog, averaged over 8 pieces of each interval, and every other term (burden, resistant cells, dose, toxicity, and in the fasting runs host weight and fasting) is multiplied by 0.1, so it only breaks ties between plans that progress at the same time. The look-ahead is fixed (no end-of-run cost), so the final count is not a target. The comparators always run the whole run. An MPC run stops computing once progression is confirmed at 6 successive decisions (10 days; the controller keeps re-planning through them to bring the culture back), or once it has gone 10.0 days past the latest comparator's progression without progressing; its time to progression is read up to that day (the CSV column control_stopped_day) and the rest of the run follows its last plan, not re-optimised. The run length is therefore a cap.

Progression rule (user, 2026-11-03): the culture counts as progressed when the resistant line reaches 0.5 K or the sensitive line reaches 0.85 × its untreated plateau (not N + C ≥ 0.5 K as before). The cost uses the smooth OR \(1-(1-\sigma_N)(1-\sigma_C)\) of the two steps.

The model the controller uses

Two controls, each held constant over a 2-day interval between media changes: the cisplatin concentration \(u\) (µM) and fasting \(v\) (1 = the interval is run in 0.5% FBS, 0 = fed, 10% FBS). Fasting enters the cells through the fitted low-resource level \(u_R = 0.9548\,v\), and the host through the weight and protection states. When \(v = 0\) the model is exactly the 14-day control model; with \(u = 1\) µM and \(v = 1\) it is exactly the fitted Stage 4 model (both checked below).

How much rests on recovery after refeeding

In the fitted low-resource model (Stage 2a), starvation stress in A2780cis builds up through a chain of compartments at rate \(k_C^R = 0.3125\)/day, and after refeeding it fades at the same slow rate (about 10 days). One 48-h fast therefore keeps killing resistant cells for weeks. This is why the controller needs so little fasting. No experiment measured recovery after refeeding: every low-resource culture stayed in 0.5% FBS for all 14 days. Here the stress is allowed to fade once fed, at rate \(k_{off}\) (the chain rate becomes \(k = k_C^R\,v + k_{off}(1-v)\)), and the same schedules are replayed and re-optimised.

Experiment that would settle it: A2780cis (and a 1:1 coculture) in 0.5% FBS for 48 h, then back to 10% FBS, counted daily for 10–14 days.

  • The fast, type-stable right-hand side used by the controller equals the zoo's generic right-hand side to 10⁻¹².
  • Fixed-step RK4 matches an adaptive Tsit5 solve (< 10⁻⁵ relative); automatic-differentiation Jacobians A = ∂F/∂x and B = ∂F/∂(u, v) match central finite differences (< 10⁻⁴ relative); the adjoint gradient matches finite differences of the cost (< 10⁻⁵ of its largest component).
  • Every stage and terminal cost is ≥ 0 at random states (what makes pruning in the exact search safe), and the cost split by term adds up to the cost.
  • The exact search equals brute force with the fasting rules, and a warm start never changes its answer; SQP keeps both controls in bounds and never raises the cost.
  • The nominal closed loop with the fixed look-ahead equals replaying its own schedule.
  • Weight, protection and toxicity follow their equations; with p_H = 0 fasting gives no protection.

What the data do not support

  • No in-vivo attenuation yet. The tumour-side effect of fasting is the in-vitro effect of 0.5% FBS at full strength. A patient's fast changes serum glucose, insulin and IGF-1 far less than a 20-fold serum cut, so the benefit shown here is an upper bound. Scaling it down is the planned next step.
  • Recovery after refeeding of starvation damage was never measured, and the result depends on it strongly (section above).
  • Days 14–30 extrapolate every stage beyond the data. The model has no passaging: a culture near its capacity K stays there, which no real culture does for 30 days, so read the late days as the long-run tendency of the fitted equations, not as a prediction for a flask.
  • In-vitro days are not patient days. Every rate here (growth, drug effect, starvation stress) is a culture rate in a dish; a 2-day fasting interval is 2 days in 0.5% FBS, and day 30 is a culture day. Mapping the schedule onto a patient's calendar would need pharmacokinetics and tumour growth rates in vivo, which none of these data provide.
  • The weight limit is an assumption. W_min = 0.95 is informed by the > 5% weight-loss criterion for cancer cachexia (Fearon et al. 2011), but that criterion is defined over 6 months, not for an acute fast; weight lost in a short fast is largely water and glycogen and is regained after refeeding. It is swept (0.90–0.98).
  • The burden limit is a soft penalty. B_cancer = 0.5 K is where a relaxed barrier (a penalty) starts to dominate the cost, not a hard cap; growing cultures pass it, the tables count whether and for how long, and its position and the terminal weight are swept.
  • Fasting without drug is predicted with an RMSE of 1386 cells (the Stage 3b fit: 565 cells on the points it used; checks above), and the drug × low-resource interaction was measured at one dose only (1 µM).
  • Host states are literature-informed proxies: nothing in these experiments measured toxicity, weight or protection. The weight rates come from a case series, the weight limit is an assumption informed by a consensus cachexia definition, and the protection strength is an assumption (the randomized DIRECT trial found less DNA damage in T cells but no difference in toxicity grades, so p_H = 0 is in the sweep).
  • Resistant kill in coculture (scenario A vs B) remains the largest structural uncertainty, as in the 14-day report. The coculture kill factors (φ) were measured at 1 µM only, the one dose the cocultures received, and are applied unchanged at every dose, so scenario A at 0.62 and 1.47 µM extrapolates them; scenario B is the bound for a resistant kill as in monoculture. Scenario B is a what-if: the monoculture drug response applied in coculture. The treated cocultures contradict it: at a constant 1 µM it predicts a day-14 total of 592 cells at 50:50 against 3310 measured (scenario A, the Stage 3a coculture fit: 3398).
  • Beyond the data. Everything past day 14 is model extrapolation: the experiments end at day 14. In low resource (0.5% FBS) the resistant line dies without any drug, in the data and in the plant. The treated low-resource plant rests on a single series, the 50:50 combination culture (Stage 4). Fasting is modelled as low resource, so every fasting result rests on that single treated low-resource series.
  • The toxicity limit is inactive at the default settings. T can never exceed a_H/r_H = 0.1 (less while fasting protects healthy tissue), below the bound B_tox = 0.25; the toxicity cost still discourages dosing, but the toxicity barrier never decides a schedule here.
  • Measurement and state estimation. The closed-loop robustness runs assume each media change gives separate counts of N and C with 10% noise and that the hidden drug-effect, stress and host states follow the planner's own model. Scenario B's bootstrap draws borrow the Stage 3a bootstrap ratios for its crowding parameters.
